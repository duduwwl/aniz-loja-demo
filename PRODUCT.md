# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary (confirmed): the owner of Aniz evaluating this site as a proposal for an online store. Success is her recognizing her shop in it and wanting it. Secondary: women from Lavras and region who follow @loja_aniz and would browse the catalog.

## Product Purpose
A static demo e-commerce for Aniz (pijamas, lingerie, moda praia, malas; Lavras – MG, since 1988). Shows the real pieces from the shop's own Instagram photos, with a working catalog, bag and fully simulated checkout. Nothing is sold or charged.

## Positioning
Built only from the shop's own photographs and confirmed facts: a store of 38 years in one town, selling sleepwear, lingerie, beachwear and travel bags side by side, with a shop window it dresses by theme (the "cãezinhos fofos" window, the Lua Luá corner).

## Operating Context
Pages: landing, catalog, product, checkout (4 steps), confirmation. Hash routing on GitHub Pages. Data centralized in `src/data/products.ts` and `src/data/store.ts`.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Prices, sizes, availability, shipping and payments are fictitious and must be labelled as such.
- Store address, hours, CNPJ, policies: not confirmed; must not be invented.
- Every image comes from public posts of @loja_aniz (see FONTES.md).

## Brand Commitments
- Keep the real shop photographs as the only imagery (user-confirmed).
- Keep the logo blue of the "✻ ANIZ" mark (user-confirmed).
- Body and display type must feel delicate, not heavy (user feedback on the previous version).
- Public demo notice stays visible.

## Evidence on Hand
30 processed photos in `public/img/` (sources in FONTES.md). Contacts: WhatsApp (35) 99244-3983, phone (35) 3822-3983, @loja_aniz, facebook.com/anizloja. No testimonials, reviews, press or sales figures exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles
- The shop's own pieces and window lead; the interface recedes.
- Truthful: every fact traceable to a public source, every invented number labelled.
- Feels like Aniz of Lavras, not a generic marketplace.
